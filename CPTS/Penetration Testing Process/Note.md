A pentest aims to uncover & identify all vulns in the system under investigation & improve security for the tested system.

**Testing Methods:** External or Internal

**Types of PT:** BlackBox, GreyBox, WhiteBox, Red Teaming, Purple Teaming

**Types of Testing Env:** Network, IoT, Hosts, Web Apps, Cloud, Server, Mobile, Source Code, Security Policies, API, Physical Security, Firewalls, Thick Clients, Employees, IDS/IPS

### Pre-engagement process consists of three components:

i. Scoping Questionnaire

ii. Pre-engagement meeting

iii. Kick off meeting

### Contract Checklist

**NDA (Non-Disclosure Agreement):** Confidentiality agreement signed before testing.

**Goals:** Define what PT should achieve.

**Scope:** Specify system, IP, domain and assets to test.

**PT Type:** Choose type.

**Methodology:** Testing standard & methods.

**Testing Location:** External, Internal & Remote.

**Time Estimation:** Set test start/end dates & testing windows.

**Third Parties:** Obtain permission from cloud providers, ISPs, hosting providers.

**Evasive Testing:** Decide whether bypassing security controls is allowed.

**Risks:** Explain possible impacts & agree on precautions.

**Scope Limitations:** Identify critical systems that must not be disrupted.

**Information Handling:** Follow compliance rules (HIPAA, PCI, NIST etc).

**Contact Information:** Keep emergency contact & information/escalation list.

**Lines of Communication:** Define communication methods.

**Reporting:** Decide report format, delivery & presentation.

**Payment Terms:** Agree on pricing & payment conditions.
