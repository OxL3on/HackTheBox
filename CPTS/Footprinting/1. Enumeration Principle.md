## **Enumeration Principles**

Enumeration is the process of gathering information about a target.

It uses two methods: Active methods, Passive methods.

**Core Principles**

| No. | Principle |
| --- | --- |
| **1** | There is **more than meets the eye.** Consider every point of view. |
| **2** | Distinguish between **what you can see** and **what you cannot see.** |
| **3** | There is **always more information to gather.** Understand the target completely. |
