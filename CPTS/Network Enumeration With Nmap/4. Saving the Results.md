## Saving the Results

**Can save results in 3 different formats.**

**Normal Output**

- oN

**Extension**: .nmap

**Grepable Output**

- oG

**Extension:** .gnmap

**XML**

- oX

**Extension:** .xml

**Save all three formats.**

- oA

**HTML Reports:**

```bash
xsltproc target.xml -o target.html
```
