## Writing Custom Wordlists and Rules

```
hashcat --force password.list -r custom.rule --stdout | sort-u > mut_pass.list
```

One of the most widely used rulesets:

```
best64.rule
```

### Generating Passwords with CeWL

Scans potential words from a company's website and saves them into a separate file.

Example:

```
cewl https://www.something.com -d 4 -m 6 --lowercase -w inlane.wordlist
```
